Exciting news for all open-source enthusiasts and contributors! The submission deadline for FOSSCOMM 2024 has been **extended (new deadline is September 25)**, giving you more time to share your innovative ideas, projects, and insights with the Free and Open-Source Software (FOSS) community.

FOSSCOMM, one of Greece’s most prominent conferences on Free and Open-Source Software, will be held in November 2024, bringing together developers, advocates, and users to explore the latest trends and advancements in open-source technologies. Whether you’re an experienced developer or new to the open-source movement, this is your chance to make a difference.

### Extended Deadline Details

The new submission deadline is **September 25, 2024**. This extension provides additional time for individuals and groups to submit proposals for presentations and workshops in a variety of FOSS-related fields.

### Topics of Interest

FOSSCOMM 2024 encourages submissions on a broad range of topics related to Free and Open-Source Software, including but not limited to:

- Software Engineering – Development
- Artificial Intelligence
- Community Networks
- DevOps
- Open Art
- Open Data
- Open Hardware
- Open Media
- Programming Languages
- Science
- Social / Policies / Legal
- Space
- System Software
- Security
- Web / Mobile technologies
- Metaverse / Augmented Reality / Virtual Reality / Mixed Reality

Submissions outside of these topics are also welcome, as long as they contribute to the FOSS landscape.

### Formats for Submissions

We accept submissions in both **Greek and English** across the following formats:

- **Presentations**: 30-minute talks followed by a Q&A session.
- **Workshops**: Interactive, hands-on sessions with flexible timeframes based on the content.

### Important Dates

- **Extended Submission Deadline**: September 25, 2024
- **Conference Dates**: November 9-10, 2024

### Why Submit?

Participating in FOSSCOMM 2024 is an excellent opportunity to:

- Showcase your work and ideas to a wide audience of FOSS enthusiasts.
- Contribute to the growth and development of the open-source ecosystem.
- Network with other professionals and advocates in the FOSS community.
- Gain recognition for your contributions to Free and Open-Source Software.

### How to Submit

Visit the official FOSSCOMM 2024 Call for Papers page to submit your proposal. Make sure to include a detailed abstract of your presentation or workshop, along with relevant background information.

Don’t miss this opportunity to be part of **FOSSCOMM 2024**! With the extended deadline, now’s the perfect time to finalize your submission and contribute to the vibrant world of open-source software.
